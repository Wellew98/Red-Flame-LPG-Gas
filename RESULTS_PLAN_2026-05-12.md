## Track 2 — Paid Ads (the only realistic path to August's lead target)

**Reality check:** Google Local Service Ads is NOT available in South Africa. The play is **Google Ads Search Campaign** targeting your highest-intent commercial queries with tight geographic targeting.

### 2.1 — Recommended campaign structure

**One campaign, three ad groups:**

1. **Refill / Delivery (high intent):**
   - Keywords: `gas refill cape town`, `gas refill woodstock`, `gas refill claremont`, `lpg refill near me`, `gas delivery cape town`, `same-day gas delivery`, `9kg gas refill`, `gas bottle delivery cape town`
   - Match type: phrase + exact only. **No broad match** — it wastes budget on irrelevant queries.

2. **Installation / COC (highest commercial intent):**
   - Keywords: `gas installer cape town`, `gas installation woodstock`, `rondebosch gas installation`, `claremont gas installation`, `gas certificate of compliance`, `gas coc cape town`, `sans 10087 install`
   - These are 1–2 click → install jobs worth R3000–R8000 each. **Highest ROI ad group.**

3. **Suburb defense:**
   - Keywords: `gas [suburb]` for every suburb page — Woodstock, Higgovale, Pinelands, Rondebosch, Atlantic Seaboard, Claremont
   - Captures suburb-name searchers Laughtons currently wins

**Negative keywords (block these):**
`stove install`, `geyser`, `solar`, `jobs`, `vacancies`, `cylinder for sale used`, `free gas`, `wholesale`, locations outside Cape Town

### 2.2 — Geographic targeting (CRITICAL)

- **Location target:** Radius 15 km from 170 Victoria Road, Woodstock
- **Exclude:** Outside Cape Town metro, Gardens (Laughtons territory — wasted spend), Salt River

### 2.3 — Budget estimate

**Realistic ZAR economics in Cape Town LPG:**

- Average CPC for commercial intent queries: **R8–R15**

### 2.4 — Conversion tracking (non-negotiable)

If you spend money on ads without tracking, you are setting it on fire.

**Minimum tracking setup:**

1. **Call tracking:** Use Google's free phone-call conversion (forwards your real number through a Google-tracked one)
2. **WhatsApp click tracking:** Add UTM parameters to the WhatsApp link on every page: `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=refill`
3. **GA4** linked to GSC and Google Ads
4. **A spreadsheet** Emmanuel fills in every Friday: "how many calls/WhatsApps this week, how many turned into a job"

Without (4), you will never know if the ads are working.

## Devil's advocate — what could make all of this wrong

1. **Laughtons could outspend you.** They have the entrenched position. If they notice your ads and start bidding aggressively on the same keywords, CPCs double and the unit economics break. Mitigation: don't compete on Laughtons-branded keywords; focus on "near me" + suburb + service-intent terms where they're less aggressive.
2. **LPG demand is seasonal in Cape Town** (winter peak May–August). Some of your impression growth is the season, not your SEO. Honest read in October will be more meaningful than the August target.
3. **Conversion rate is the wildest variable.** I quoted 8%. If your WhatsApp response time slips past 10 minutes during the day, expect 3–4% instead, and the math breaks. Hire/train someone to watch WhatsApp during business hours, or this whole plan loses 40% of its expected output.
4. **The ad strategy assumes Google approves your account.** New advertisers in service categories sometimes get held in review for 7–14 days. Build a 2-week buffer into the timeline.
