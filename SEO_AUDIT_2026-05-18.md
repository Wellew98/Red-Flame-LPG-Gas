## CRITICAL — fix this week (drives the 0-click number directly)

### 1. The local pack is eating your clicks, not your titles

For every query in your screenshot — `gas refill near me`, `gas refill woodstock`, `gas refill claremont`, `gas bottle delivery cape town`, `cape town gas delivery` — Google shows the **Map Pack** (3-business map result) above the organic listings. Your GBP profile sits in or near that pack (614 monthly views proves it). When someone clicks, they click the map result, not the blue organic link. The website is then a fallback.

**Implication:** No amount of website tweaking moves the click number meaningfully until the GBP side converts better. The website earns clicks for queries the map pack DOESN'T dominate (informational queries like *"gas certificate of compliance"* or *"sans 10087 home"*) and as a secondary tap from the GBP listing.

**Action — GBP is the highest-leverage fix on this whole audit:**
- Click **"Complete info"** on the profile-strength prompt (currently not 100%). Whatever's missing — services list, attributes, FAQ, opening hours per service — fill it.
- Add **photos every week for the next 4 weeks**. GBP weighting on photo recency is heavy. Cylinder photos, delivery vehicle, installer at work, the shop front, COC certificate (close-up).
- Post a **GBP Post** weekly — "Same-day refill in [suburb] today", an offer, a service highlight. Posts decay after 7 days; treat them like a feed.
- **Review velocity** — you have 11 reviews at 5.0 ⭐. Competitors in Cape Town LPG have 50–200+. Use the "Ask for reviews" tile in your GBP dashboard. Target: +5 reviews/month for the next 3 months.

### 2. All 7 suburb-page schema breadcrumbs still say "Gas Delivery"

You shifted page titles to "Gas Refill" / "Gas Refill & Installation" on 2026-05-12 to chase the higher-volume keyword, but the BreadcrumbList JSON-LD on every suburb page **still says** `"name":"Gas Delivery [Suburb]"`:

```
atlantic-seaboard.html:70  Gas Delivery Atlantic Seaboard
claremont.html:60          Gas Delivery Claremont
higgovale.html:53          Gas Delivery Higgovale
newlands.html:60           Gas Delivery Newlands
pinelands.html:53          Gas Delivery Pinelands
rondebosch.html:53         Gas Delivery Rondebosch
woodstock.html:60          Gas Delivery Woodstock
```

Google reads the BreadcrumbList schema and **uses it in the SERP breadcrumb display under the title**. So your SERP listing for "gas refill woodstock" reads: title says "Gas Refill Woodstock", breadcrumb says "Gas Delivery Woodstock". Mixed signal, weaker click-through.

**Action:** Update each `BreadcrumbList` position-2 `name` to match its new title pattern (e.g. `"Gas Refill Woodstock"`, `"Gas Refill & Installation Pinelands"`). 7-file find/replace. Oranjezicht and Edgemead already match because they were rebuilt 2026-05-12; double-check during the same pass.

### 3. The `_upload/` folder is a duplicate-content time bomb

You have a full second copy of the website inside `_upload/`:

```
_upload/index.html              — title "Red Flame Gas Supplier, Free Gas Delivery Cape Town"
_upload/index.html              — meta description still includes R175/R350/R515/R700/R1600 prices
_upload/woodstock.html          — title "Gas Delivery Woodstock | …"
_upload/atlantic-seaboard.html  — old "Gas Delivery" titles
… 7 stale pages total
```

Worse: **the `_upload` pages have canonicals pointing to the LIVE URLs** (`canonical = https://www.redflamegas.co.za/woodstock` etc.). If Cloudflare Pages serves these at `/_upload/woodstock.html` (likely, since underscored folders aren't auto-excluded), Google can crawl them, see they self-canonicalize to the live URL, and your stale meta could leak into the cached SERP listing. The `_upload/index.html` meta still advertises prices you pulled 25 days ago.

**Action — pick one:**
- **Cleanest:** delete the entire `_upload/` folder. It looks like a backup snapshot from May 1; you have git or the live site as the source of truth.
- **If you want to keep the backup:** add `_upload` to `robots.txt` as `Disallow: /_upload/` AND add a `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` to each file in there. Belt and braces.

### 4. Three suburb pages have H1/title mismatch

| Page | Title | H1 |
|---|---|---|
| pinelands.html | Gas Refill **& Installation** Pinelands | Gas Refill **& Delivery** in Pinelands |
| higgovale.html | Gas Refill **& Installation** Higgovale | Gas Refill **& Delivery** in Higgovale |

**Action:** Update the H1 on those 3 pages to mirror the title — `"Gas Refill & Installation in Pinelands"`, etc.

## HIGH — fix this month

### 5. Homepage H1 is missing the geo modifier

Current H1: `Fast, Free Gas Refill & Delivery`. Missing "Cape Town". You're ranking for `gas refill cape town` (4 imp/0 clicks) and `cape town gas delivery` (8 imp/0 clicks) — both moderately competitive — with an H1 that doesn't contain the city name. Title and meta have it; H1 doesn't.

**Action:** Change to `Fast, Free Gas Refill & Delivery in Cape Town` or similar. Geo modifier in the H1 lifts both relevance and SERP snippet quality (Google sometimes uses H1 as snippet seed when the meta loses bidding).

The Pinelands one and the Higgovale one feel interchangeable.

**Action:** Differentiate by suburb pain point:

- **Higgovale:** "Free delivery up the Tafelberg slopes, including the steepest stretches."
- **Rondebosch:** "COC installations for student flats, family homes and conveyancing-ready transfers."
- **Atlantic Seaboard:** "Same-day delivery from Sea Point to Bakoven."
- **Claremont:** "Gas installations and refills for Southern Suburbs homes and restaurants."
- **Pinelands:** "Refill and install for Pinelands homes — same-day from our Woodstock shop."

Each ends with the WhatsApp number per your existing rule.
