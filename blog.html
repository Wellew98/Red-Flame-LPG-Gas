Blog FAQ

Questions about the blog.

How often do you publish new gas advice?
Weekly. One post every week with an audio version on the page, starting with the Cape Town refill price guide. Every installation we write about is built to SANS 10087 with the Gas Certificate of Compliance (COC) issued on the same visit.
Is gas delivery really free?
Yes. Delivery is free from our Woodstock shop to Higgovale, Pinelands, Rondebosch, Atlantic Seaboard and the wider Cape Town City Bowl. No minimum order on refills, no hidden fees. Outside our named suburbs, WhatsApp us. We usually still deliver.
How much does a gas refill cost in Cape Town?
A gas refill in Cape Town costs R240 for 5 kg, R399 for 9 kg, R610 for 14 kg, R799 for 19 kg and R1,850 for 48 kg, VAT included, with free same-day delivery in our delivery area. Those prices are for refilling or exchanging a cylinder you already own, and any mainstream brand is swapped. The national maximum LPG price is reviewed monthly, so WhatsApp us to confirm before you order.
Do you issue a gas Certificate of Compliance (COC)?
Yes. A gas COC is legally required under SANS 10087 when a property changes hands in South Africa. We issue the Certificate of Compliance on every installation, so insurers, estate agents and new owners have the document they need.
