What a gas geyser installation involves.

Running the gas line from the cylinder or bank to the geyser, fitting the flue for safe exhaust venting, connecting the water inlet and outlet, pressure testing the full system for leaks, and signing the Gas Certificate of Compliance once everything passes. Internal geysers need proper ventilation checked against SANS 10087. External units mounted on an outside wall are the simpler and more common installation in Cape Town, from Woodstock and the City Bowl to the Atlantic Seaboard and the Southern Suburbs.

Which cylinder size you need.

19kg is the minimum for a single-bathroom household running a gas geyser. Larger households, or homes with more than one bathroom, usually need a 48kg cylinder or a bank of two.

19kg single cylinder

The minimum size for a single-bathroom household. It runs the geyser, but you are on a tighter refill schedule and more exposed to running dry mid-shower on a cold morning.

48kg cylinder, or a bank of two

Recommended for larger households or more than one bathroom. Two cylinders with an auto-changeover valve mean the geyser switches to the second bottle automatically when the first runs dry, so you WhatsApp us for a refill on your own schedule instead of an emergency one.

Where a gas geyser can actually go.

A flue to the outside and permanent ventilation, every time

A gas geyser burns air and vents combustion gases, including carbon monoxide, so where it can go is decided by flueing and ventilation rather than by convenience. It can go in a bathroom, but only where it is flued to the outside and the room has permanent ventilation that nobody can close off. What it cannot do is sit sealed inside a cupboard or in a room with no fixed air supply. That is what catches out renovations, because a sealed cupboard is usually exactly where the old electric geyser sat.

Worth confirming before the cupboard or the tiling goes in, not after. If you are planning a renovation, send us the layout and we will tell you where the geyser and its flue can go, and what ventilation that spot needs.

What moves the price.

  • Internal or external mounting. An internal unit needs a balanced flue and more ventilation work.
  • Cylinder size. A single 19kg cylinder is cheaper to set up than a 48kg bank with auto-changeover.
  • Flue routing. The further the flue has to run to a safe outside point, the more it costs.
  • Existing pipework. Old pipe or an unsafe previous installation gets quoted separately.
  • Access. A ground-floor Woodstock flat is quicker than a third-floor unit with no lift.
  • Distance from the cage to the geyser. Every extra metre of gas line adds material and labour.

What size gas cylinder does a gas geyser need?
19kg minimum for a single-bathroom household, 48kg for larger households or more than one bathroom. We recommend two cylinders with auto-changeover so hot water never runs out mid-shower. Red Flame supplies and refills both sizes from our Woodstock shop.
Does a gas geyser work during load shedding?
Yes. A gas geyser heats water on LPG, not mains electricity, so it keeps working through every load shedding stage. It is one of the few home appliances that is fully unaffected by the grid.
Do I need a COC for a gas geyser?
Yes. A gas geyser installation is a permanent LPG connection under SANS 10087 and legally requires a Gas Certificate of Compliance. We issue it on the same visit once the installation passes the pressure test.
How long does a gas geyser installation take?
Typically half a day for a standard installation, including the gas line, flue, water connection, pressure test and the paperwork for the COC. We confirm a time window when you WhatsApp the job details.
