When It Is the Cylinder, Not the Geyser.

Check the Cylinder Before You Assume It's Broken

A large share of winter "geyser is broken" callouts turn out to be an almost-empty or undersized cylinder, not a fault in the unit at all. Low gas pressure from a near-empty bottle mimics an ignition fault or a geyser that cuts out mid-shower. If your cylinder is 19kg and you're running a geyser plus a hob, or the household has grown since it was sized, a bigger cylinder or a second one on auto-changeover is often the cheaper fix, and it's one we can arrange from the same Woodstock shop as the repair callout.

Servicing

Annual Servicing.

A yearly service checks the burner and ignition assembly, the flue, every gas connection and the water pressure feeding the unit, catching a small fault before it becomes a mid-winter breakdown. Many household insurance policies require an annual service on a gas appliance to keep cover valid, worth checking your policy wording if you haven't had one done.

Compliance

Repairs and Your COC.

A like-for-like part replacement, a new pilot assembly or a valve swap, usually does not affect an existing Gas Certificate of Compliance. A repair that changes the installation itself, a new regulator, altered piping or a relocated cylinder, does require recertifying. We tell you on the callout if the repair affects your COC, and can issue a new one on the same visit. See our Gas Certificate of Compliance page for the full detail.

Frequently Asked

Gas Geyser Repair Questions Cape Town Customers Ask.

Why is my gas geyser not igniting?
The most common causes are an empty or near-empty cylinder, a closed or faulty gas valve, a fouled pilot or ignition sensor, or low water pressure not triggering the flow switch. We diagnose on the callout, before assuming a part is broken, because an empty cylinder is by far the most common cause in winter.
How much does a gas geyser repair cost in Cape Town?
Callout and fault diagnosis is from R750, including labour. Parts and any remedial repair are quoted on site once the fault is identified, so you know the cost before we proceed.
How often should a gas geyser be serviced?
Annually is standard, and many household insurance policies require it. A service checks the burner, ignition, flue, gas connections and water pressure before small issues become a mid-winter breakdown.
Do I need a new COC after a geyser repair?
Only if the repair changed the gas installation itself, a new regulator, altered piping or a relocated cylinder. A like-for-like part replacement usually does not require a new Certificate of Compliance. We tell you on the callout if the repair affects your existing COC.
Can you repair a geyser you did not install?
Yes. We repair and service gas geysers regardless of who installed them, provided the unit is a standard LPG gas geyser.
