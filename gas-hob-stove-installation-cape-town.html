What's Involved

What a Hob or Stove Installation Involves.

Running the gas line from the cylinder to the appliance, fitting the regulator and isolation valve, checking clearances around the burners, pressure testing the connection for leaks, and signing the Gas Certificate of Compliance once it passes. A single hob connection in a Woodstock or City Bowl flat is usually the simplest install we do; a freestanding stove or a kitchen renovation with a moved gas point takes longer.

Cylinder Setup

Internal or External Cylinder Setup.

A 9kg cylinder inside a ventilated kitchen cupboard is the standard setup for a single hob in a small flat or apartment, common across Woodstock and the City Bowl. A freestanding stove, a double hob, or a kitchen that also feeds a braai point usually needs an external cylinder or bank instead, mounted in a gas cage outside where it can be swapped without anyone entering the house. We assess the kitchen on site and recommend whichever setup actually suits how it gets used.

Kitchen Safety

The 200mm Rule.

No Plug Points Within 200mm of a Burner or Gas Release Point

SANS 10087 requires at least 200mm of clearance between any plug point or light switch and a gas burner or release point, and no plug points at all in the same partition as an internal cylinder. This trips up a lot of DIY kitchen renovations, an electrician places a socket exactly where the hob was meant to go, and the installation fails compliance before it starts. Worth checking before tiling or cabinetry goes in, not after.

Frequently Asked

Hob & Stove Questions Cape Town Customers Ask.

How much does a gas hob installation cost?
From R2,500 for a standard installation, including the COC. Excludes the hob or stove itself, cylinders and a cage if one is required. WhatsApp your kitchen layout for an exact quote.
Can a 9kg cylinder go inside a kitchen cupboard?
Yes, provided the cupboard is properly ventilated and no plug points or light switches sit in the same partition as the cylinder. This is a standard setup for a single hob in a small Cape Town kitchen.
Do I need a COC for a gas hob?
Yes. Any permanently connected gas hob or stove installation requires a Gas Certificate of Compliance under SANS 10087. We issue it on the same visit once the installation passes the pressure test.
Can you connect a stove I already bought?
Yes. We install any hob or stove brand, whether you bought it yourself or we supply one. The installation, gas line and COC are the same job either way.
How long does a hob installation take?
Usually 1 to 2 hours for a single hob or stove connection, including the pressure test and the COC paperwork.
