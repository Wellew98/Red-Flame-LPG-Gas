Certificate of Compliance

Every Installation Comes with a Gas Certificate of Compliance.

A gas COC is a legal requirement under SANS 10087 for every new LPG installation in South Africa, and it is compulsory on a property sale. Only an installer registered with SAQCC Gas may issue one. We pressure test the line, check every joint and connection, and sign the COC on the same visit once the installation passes, so you are not waiting on a second appointment for the paperwork. Need a COC on an existing installation instead of a new one? See our dedicated COC page.

Cylinder Setup

Internal or External Installation?

A cupboard-integrated 9kg cylinder suits a single hob in a small kitchen and keeps the installation compact, but it caps you at one small bottle with no plug points allowed in the same partition. An external installation, cylinder or bank mounted in a ventilated gas cage outside, is what a geyser, fireplace, heater or multi-appliance kitchen needs: it takes 19kg to 48kg cylinders, refills without anyone entering the house, and scales to two cylinders with an auto-changeover so you are never caught with an empty bottle mid-job. We assess the kitchen or appliance on site and recommend the setup that matches how the property is actually used, not the cheapest option on paper.

What are the rules for installing gas in South Africa?
LPG gas installations in South Africa must be built to SANS 10087 and installed by an installer registered with SAQCC Gas. Once the installation is complete, the installer pressure tests it and issues a Gas Certificate of Compliance (COC). An installation without a valid COC is not legally compliant and will hold up a property sale or an insurance claim.
Do I need a COC for a 9kg gas bottle?
If the 9kg cylinder is permanently connected to an appliance such as a hob, stove or geyser, yes, that installation needs a COC. A 9kg bottle used loose on a portable braai or camping stove does not need one.
Who is allowed to issue a gas Certificate of Compliance?
Only an installer registered with SAQCC Gas may issue a valid Gas Certificate of Compliance. A COC signed by someone without current SAQCC registration is not valid and will not be accepted by insurers, estate agents or the authorities. Red Flame Gas Supplier is SAQCC registered.
How long does an installation take?
A single hob or stove connection usually takes 1 to 2 hours. A geyser, fireplace or full cylinder bank installation typically takes half a day, including the pressure test and paperwork for the COC. We confirm a time window when you WhatsApp the job details.
