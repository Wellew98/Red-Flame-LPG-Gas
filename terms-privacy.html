6. Gas installations and the Certificate of Compliance (COC)

We carry out domestic and commercial LPG gas installations — hobs, stoves, geysers, cylinder banks and connections — to the South African standard SANS 10087. On completion of every installation we issue a Gas Certificate of Compliance (COC).

A Certificate of Compliance certifies that the gas installation, as inspected and pressure-tested on the date of issue, conforms to SANS 10087. A valid COC is required by law when a property containing a gas installation is sold or transferred, and is commonly requested by insurers. The certificate reflects the condition of the installation at the date of issue only.

The COC covers the installation work performed by Red Flame. It does not cover: alterations, additions or repairs made by anyone else after the certificate is issued; damage, tampering or misuse after issue; or appliances we did not install or inspect. Any change to a certified installation requires a fresh inspection and a new COC.

After installation you are responsible for using the installation correctly, keeping it maintained, and reporting any suspected gas leak immediately. If you smell gas: close the cylinder valve, ventilate the area, avoid flames and electrical switches, and contact us or an emergency service.

7. Safety

LPG is a flammable fuel and must be handled with care. Always use approved cylinders, regulators and hoses; keep cylinders upright and ventilated; do not store cylinders indoors below ground level or near heat or ignition sources; and have gas installations carried out and certified by a registered installer. We are not liable for incidents arising from misuse, unauthorised modification, or equipment we did not supply or install.

Stop-work for unsafe conditions. If, while delivering or carrying out an installation or connection, we identify an unsafe condition — for example a gas leak, a damaged hose, pigtail or regulator, an unsafe or non-standard installation, signs of tampering, or unsafe cylinder storage — we will stop and decline to connect or proceed until the problem has been put right by a suitably qualified person. This is for your safety and is not negotiable.
